# EDUCATIONAL USE, FICTIONAL SCENARIO AND ETHICAL LIMITATIONS

**EchoTrace — The Missing Evidence 2: The Phantom Restore**

**EXCLUSIVELY FOR EDUCATIONAL, TRAINING AND AUTHORIZED DIGITAL-FORENSICS RESEARCH PURPOSES.**

This scenario is a synthetic case study for cybersecurity education, incident-response exercises, forensic evidence analysis, and controlled laboratory assessment. It is **not** a report of an actual cyberattack.

- The enterprise, employees, users, hosts, incidents, communications, data, credentials, records and alleged attacker actions are fictional or synthetic.
- Any resemblance to actual persons, companies or events is coincidental and unintended.
- Documentation IP ranges and reserved example domains are used as simulation identifiers, not real destinations.
- Students must use only authorized systems and isolated virtual machines. Do not access, scan, disrupt, or test third-party infrastructure.
- No real personal, customer, financial, health, or production data is authorized for inclusion.
- The artifacts must not include functional malware, stolen credentials or destructive payloads.
- A simulated indicator does not prove real compromise; evidence must be interpreted within the training case.
- Allegations of automation or AI assistance must be justified from artifacts, never presumed.
- Student packages must exclude answer keys, instructor notes and private walkthroughs.
- The repository and scenario are not endorsed, approved or published by Hack The Box unless separately confirmed by the platform.
